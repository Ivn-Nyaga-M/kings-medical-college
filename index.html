Who We Are

A focused, practical medical training college in the heart of Nyeri.

King's Medical College trains students for real careers in Kenya's health sector, combining classroom instruction with hands-on practice in our clinical skills lab, computer lab and campus facilities. Our programs are built around the qualifications employers in clinical, nursing and mortuary science fields actually look for.

From Diploma to Certificate level — and bridging courses for students who need to strengthen their sciences first — there's a clear pathway into a healthcare career here.
