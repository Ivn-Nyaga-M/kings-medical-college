Campus Life

A campus built for focused, practical learning

Home / Campus Life

Clinical Skills Lab

Practice on real equipment before real placements.

Our clinical skills lab gives students a safe, supervised space to practice patient care procedures on training equipment before moving into supervised clinical placements - building confidence and competence side by side.

Computer Lab

Digital literacy built into every program.

Research, coursework and digital skills are part of the learning experience from day one. Our computer lab gives students hands-on access to the digital tools they'll need throughout their studies and careers.

Study Room

Quiet, light-filled space for independent study.

Between lectures and practicals, students use our study room for revision, reading and assignments. Long window desks give plenty of natural light, and the calm setting helps students stay focused ahead of their exams.

Campus Hall

A shared space for assembly, events and reflection.

Our campus hall hosts assemblies, orientation sessions, guest talks and gatherings throughout the academic year - a central space for the college community to come together.

Student Services

Support, right from your first visit.

Our front office team handles enquiries, admissions support and day-to-day student services - usually the first friendly face you'll meet on campus.

Student Life

A close-knit community of future health workers

From class time in the computer lab to breaks in the courtyard, students learn, study and unwind together on a calm, green campus.
