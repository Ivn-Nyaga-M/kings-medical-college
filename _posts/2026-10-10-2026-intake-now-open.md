Applications for our 2026 intake are now open. If you've been considering a career in healthcare, now is the time to apply.

## Programs available

We offer a range of diploma and certificate programs designed to prepare you for real roles in Kenya's healthcare sector:

- Diploma in Clinical Medicine and Surgery
- Diploma in Community Health Nursing
- Diploma in Mortuary Science
- Diploma in Community Health
- Certificate in Community Health
- Certificate in Healthcare Support Service Assistant
- Certificate in Caregiving
- Certificate in Mortuary Science
- Bridging Course (Maths / Chemistry / Biology / English)

## Entry requirements

Diploma programs require a minimum of Grade C- (minus) at KCSE or an equivalent qualification. If you don't yet meet the entry grade, our Bridging Course can help you qualify.

## How to apply

Applications are handled entirely online. Fill in the [application form](/admissions.html) with your details and program of interest, and our admissions team will get back to you shortly to guide you through the next steps.

We're based in Lusoi/Nyange, off Narumoru Road, Nyeri - [get in touch](/contact.html) if you have any questions before applying.
