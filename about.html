Our Story

A Nyeri-based college focused on practical health training.

King's Medical College was established to meet a clear need in Kenya's health sector: professionals who are trained not just in theory, but in the real, hands-on skills that clinics, hospitals and communities depend on. Our campus in Nyeri houses classrooms, a clinical skills lab, computer lab and dedicated staff facilities built around that goal.

Every program we run - from our diploma courses to our shorter certificate and bridging programs - is built around giving students a direct, practical pathway into employment in Kenya's healthcare system.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, hands-on medical and healthcare training that equips students with the practical skills, professional ethics and confidence to serve Kenya's healthcare sector.

Our Vision

To be a leading medical training college recognized across Kenya for producing competent, compassionate and job-ready healthcare professionals.
